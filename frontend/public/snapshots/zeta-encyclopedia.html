Dehydration

Dehydration occurs when the body loses more fluid than it takes in. Common symptoms include thirst, dry mouth, dark urine, fatigue, dizziness, and, in severe cases, confusion.

Typical signs

  • Thirst and dry mucous membranes
  • Reduced urine output or dark urine
  • Headache, light-headedness, or fatigue

Sources: World Health Organization; Canadian Medical Association patient guidance.
