Personal income tax: filing deadlines

The deadline to file your 2025 return is 30 April 2026. If you are self-employed, you have until 15 June 2026, but any tax owed is still due by 30 April.

Late filing

A late-filing penalty of 5% of the balance owing applies, plus 1% for each full month the return is late, up to 12 months.
