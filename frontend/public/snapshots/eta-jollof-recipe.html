Classic party jollof rice

By Ruth Ade · Serves 6 · 1 hour 10 minutes

Advertisement

Ingredients

  • 3 cups long-grain parboiled rice
  • 6 plum tomatoes, 2 red bell peppers, 1 scotch bonnet, 2 onions
  • 3 tbsp tomato paste, 1/3 cup vegetable oil
  • 2 cups chicken stock, 2 bay leaves, 1 tsp curry powder, 1 tsp dried thyme, salt

Method

  1. Blend the tomatoes, peppers and one onion until smooth.
  2. Fry the other onion, sliced, in the oil until soft. Add the tomato paste and fry for 5 minutes.
  3. Add the blended base and cook down for 20 minutes, until the oil rises to the top.
  4. Stir in the stock, seasoning and washed rice. Cover tightly with foil and a lid.
  5. Cook on low heat for 30–35 minutes without stirring, until the rice is tender.

Tip: a little browning at the bottom of the pot gives the smoky "party" flavour people love.
