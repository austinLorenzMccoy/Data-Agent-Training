Squeaky door hinge — how do I fix it?

tobi_88: My bedroom door squeaks every time it opens. Any quick fix?
Advertisement
handyhelen: Lift the hinge pin out with a screwdriver and a hammer, wipe it, coat it with a little petroleum jelly or white lithium grease, and tap it back in. Works every time.
dave_k: A spray lubricant on the hinge works too, but wipe off the drips so they don't stain the floor.
tobi_88: Did the pin trick, squeak is gone. Thanks!
